Strategy Overview

Blue chip stocks are shares of well-established companies with a history of reliable performance, strong financials, and market leadership. These companies typically have large market capitalizations, stable earnings, and often pay regular dividends.

Selection Criteria

  • Market Cap > ₹50,000 Crores
  • Consistent profitability for 10+ years
  • Strong brand recognition and market position
  • Regular dividend payments
  • Low debt-to-equity ratio (< 1.0)
  • ROE > 15% consistently
